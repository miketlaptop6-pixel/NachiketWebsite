01. Data Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when creating a professional resume, including but not limited to your full name, contact details, work history, educational background, and technical proficiencies.

02. Data Usage

The data we collect is used primarily to facilitate the generation of professional documents. We utilize advanced algorithms to format your data into premium templates that optimize for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

Additionally, we use your data to:

  • Improve platform performance and UX [INTERNAL]
  • Personalize template recommendations [ALGORITHMIC]
  • Facilitate account security protocols [SECURITY]

03. Cookies & Tracking

ResumeHub.in uses cookies to enhance your navigation experience and maintain persistent sessions. Our cookies fall into three categories: Essential (strictly necessary for the site to function), Analytical (to understand visitor behavior), and Functional (to remember your preferences).

Security Commitment

We employ enterprise-grade encryption for all data at rest and in transit. Your resumes are stored on secure, partitioned servers with restricted access controls. We do not sell your personal data to third-party recruiters without your explicit, opt-in consent.
