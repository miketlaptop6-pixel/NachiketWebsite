Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from Handshake or VMock?
Handshake manages employers, job postings, and events. VMock focuses on resume scoring. NachiketBhogawar.in supports the work that happens before any of that. It shows what students are actually doing in their job search, improves resume quality at scale, and gives advisors and leadership visibility into readiness and outcomes.
Will this replace advisors or career coaches?
No. NachiketBhogawar.in reduces low-value editing tasks so advisors can focus on coaching, interpretation, and strategy. It highlights who needs help and why, which increases advisor impact rather than replacing it.
What data does the Career Center receive?
Career Centers see resume activity, tailoring behavior, application patterns, inactivity, and trends by program or cohort. Advisors can request access to resumes directly within the platform. All visibility respects institutional privacy and FERPA requirements.
Do students need to learn another new tool?
No. Students already use NachiketBhogawar.in independently. They build and tailor their resumes in the same interface, and the Career Center gains visibility without asking students to change their workflow.
How quickly can we implement NachiketBhogawar.in?
Most campuses can activate a pilot in under one week. Cohorts can be invited immediately, and advisors can begin reviewing resumes as soon as activity starts.
Do students pay?
No. Students covered under an institutional license do not pay.
Does this work for online, international, and adult learners?
Yes. NachiketBhogawar.in supports multiple resume formats, asynchronous feedback, and varied learner schedules. It is already used by global and online student populations.
Can we integrate this into courses or career modules?
Yes. NachiketBhogawar.in can be embedded into FYE courses, bootcamps, capstones, internship classes, and career readiness programming to standardize quality and support faculty partnerships.
Can we run a pilot?
Yes. Pilots are available for qualified institutions. Contact our team to explore options.
